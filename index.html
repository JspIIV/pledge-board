GenLayer Asimov

A public promise, checked against its own source when the time comes.

Anyone can make a dated promise and name the page where its truth will show. After the deadline, anyone can check it: the contract fetches that page and a round of GenLayer validators decides whether it was KEPT or BROKEN. The verdict is permanent and it accrues to the author, so a record of what someone actually delivered builds up next to what they said.
