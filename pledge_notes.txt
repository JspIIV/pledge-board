Organisations make dated promises, like shipping by a date or publishing an audit. The promise is loud, the day it comes due is quiet, so whether it was kept is left to whoever remembers to look.

Pledge does the looking. pledge(commitment, source_url, due) records a promise in plain words, one public page where its truth will show, and a deadline; the author is bound to the caller. After the deadline, check(id), open to anybody, fetches that page itself and a GenLayer round decides KEPT, BROKEN or UNCLEAR. The verdict is permanent and accrues to the author, so record(address) builds a track record of promises met and missed, judged from sources not self-reported.

It never judges early: check is refused until the deadline. An unreadable page is UNCLEAR, and the deciding evidence is the page the contract fetched.

Proven on Asimov with a live board: a promise its source shows kept is KEPT; one its source shows broken is BROKEN; a promise still in its window cannot be checked yet.
